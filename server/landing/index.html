Personal agent on Fly.io Sprites

Petitio

Your flight was cancelled and the airline offers a voucher. Forward the email to Petitio. It checks the airline's excuse against official records, works out what the law says you are owed, and follows up until they pay.

How it thinks

  1. Reads the airline's email you forward, and saves the facts to its case file.
  2. Checks every claim with a tool. "Bad weather"? It pulls the official airport weather reports (METAR) for that hour.
  3. Calculates the amount from EU Regulation 261/2004 in code, never from the language model.
  4. Asks you before it sends anything to the airline or answers an offer.
  5. Follows up by email and by phone, and tells you on Telegram what it did.

A real run

📩 email from owner: "Your flight DA123 has been cancelled"
🤔 the airline blames adverse weather, check the official observations
🔧 get_metar LFPG 06:00-09:00 UTC
   ↳ 6 reports: no storm, snow or fog observed, visibility at least 10 km.
🔧 compute_compensation
   ↳ 1062.5 km flight: 250 EUR is due.
💬 "The weather excuse does not hold up. The 150 EUR voucher is below
    the 250 EUR you are owed. Shall I refuse it?"

Built on Sprites

  • Sprite runs the agent, its memory and this page, and sleeps when idle.
  • Ollama connector for the model that decides which tool to use.
  • AgentMail connector for the agent's own inbox.
  • ElevenLabs connector for phone calls with the airline.
  • Custom API connector for Telegram updates.

No provider key lives on the Sprite: every call goes through the Sprites gateway.
